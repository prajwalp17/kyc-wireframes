Key Terms

Quick reference before diving into the flows.

Term What It Means
UAE PASS The UAE's national digital identity app — the government's single sign-on. One app to prove who you are across all government and private services.
Digital Vault (DV) Secure document storage inside UAE PASS. Official documents (Emirates ID, passport, visa) are stored as digitally signed credentials. We request documents from here instead of asking users to upload photos.
SOP Levels The user's verification tier in UAE PASS.
SOP1 (Basic): Mobile + email only. Can log in, can't share documents.
SOP2 (Advanced): Verified via Emirates ID PIN. Still no Digital Vault.
SOP3 (Verified): Biometric verification done. Required for Digital Vault access.
KYC Know Your Customer — regulatory requirement to verify identity before offering financial products. We do this by requesting Emirates ID + Passport via Digital Vault.
Service Provider (SP) That's us — any company integrating with UAE PASS. We get API credentials and go through an onboarding/approval process with TDRA (the government body that runs UAE PASS).
Two Core Flows
Flow 1 — Authentication
Connecting the user's UAE PASS account to your app. Uses OAuth 2.0. You get their verified identity (name, Emirates ID number, mobile, email). This is a one-time setup — you store the link and don't need to do it again.
Covered in → Journey 1
Flow 2 — KYC via Digital Vault
Requesting actual documents (Emirates ID, Passport) from the user's Digital Vault for identity verification. The user gets a push notification in UAE PASS and approves sharing. Documents arrive at your server with digital signatures.
Covered in → Journey 2
These are two separate interactions. Authentication gives you the user's profile. Digital Vault gives you their actual documents. For a first-time user, both happen back-to-back (Journey 3).
